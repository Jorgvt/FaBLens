Start with the heroes

  1. Open a match and enable FaB Lens with the icon at the top-right of the video, the Chrome toolbar icon, or Alt+Shift+F.
  2. Open Heroes and choose the left and right heroes. Choose Top / bottom if needed. Selecting both removes cards incompatible with both heroes.
  3. Hover over a white outline to preview the card. Click to keep the preview open.
  4. Drag the preview header to move it. Resize with +, − or its bottom-right corner. Press Esc to close.
  5. If the match is wrong, click Not this card? to cycle through alternatives.

For the next game, use New match · reset heroes. Automatic hero selection is optional. The class and talent filter is not a complete deck-legality validator.

When something does not match

Choose the highest available video quality. Foil glare, dice, overlaps and small cards can still cause errors. The first scan loads the local models and may take longer.

If capture fails, click the extension's Chrome toolbar icon to grant temporary capture access, then enable it again. Protected or unavailable video may not be capturable. If you just installed or updated the extension, reload the video page.

Disable FaB Lens with the same activation button. Scanning pauses when the page is hidden. An unchanged paused frame is not scanned repeatedly.

How new cards arrive

This version includes 18,685 visual references. It does not automatically crawl Card Vault, Fabrary or Talishar. New cards and alternate arts require a rebuilt catalogue in a new extension release. Chrome Web Store installations receive published extension updates through Chrome; unpacked installations must be replaced manually.

Recognition runs locally. Large card previews still need an internet connection.
