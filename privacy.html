FAB LENS · 0.9.1

Privacy

Effective September 29, 2026 · FaB Lens 0.9.1

Local video processing

When enabled, FaB Lens reads frames from the visible video on supported YouTube and Twitch pages to locate and recognize cards. If direct video capture is unavailable, it can use Chrome temporary active-tab access to capture the visible tab and crop it to the video area. The temporary capture can contain other visible page content before cropping. Frames are processed in the browser and are not uploaded by FaB Lens.

Information used on your device

Hero choices, card matches and temporary frame signatures are used locally for matching. Page URL changes are checked locally to reset the match. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, developer telemetry or developer-operated collection servers in this build. FaB Lens does not sell data or use it for advertising.

Card image requests

The extension loads and preloads card previews from Legend Story Studios' public Amazon S3 card host, Talishar's CardImages repository on raw.githubusercontent.com, and d2wlb52bya4y8z.cloudfront.net. These image hosts receive normal network information, including your IP address and the requested card-image path. Image requests set a no-referrer policy. Video frames, hero selections and page URLs are not attached to those requests by FaB Lens. These providers operate under their own policies.

Storage and retention

The packaged recognition index is stored with the extension. This build may read eligible card-reference vectors previously stored by older versions in its local IndexedDB database. It does not write new reference vectors during normal recognition. Working match data remains in memory; image responses may be cached by the browser. Reloading closes the page's match session; disabling stops recognition. Uninstalling removes extension-owned storage. Browser and site caches are managed separately in Chrome.

Your controls

You can disable recognition, change or clear the selected heroes, manage site access in Chrome, or uninstall the extension. No registration is required.

Contact

Use the publisher contact information displayed on the Chrome Web Store listing for FaB Lens.
